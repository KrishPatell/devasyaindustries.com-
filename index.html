ABOUT US

Two Decades of Steel Innovation

Devasya Group, established in 2003, is one of India's leading manufacturers and suppliers of Stainless Steel, Alloy Steel and Carbon Steel products. We specialize in producing a wide range of Stainless Steel products that include stainless steel wires, bright bars round, hexagonal, square, flat, and custom profiles. We also have capabilities to manufacture Carbon and Alloy Steel in various shapes and profiles.

At Devasya, quality is more than a promise—it's embedded in every stage of our manufacturing process. For over two decades, we have consistently delivered authentic and certified steel trusted worldwide.

– Uncompromising Quality Standards – ISO 9001 certified, with every step of production monitored and tested.
– Certified Raw Materials – Only tested and approved inputs, because strength begins at the foundation.

Our six state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, including a dedicated Rolling Mill, span over 20,000 sq. m. and have a combined annual production capacity of approximately 25,000 MT. Engineered for excellence, our alloy steel products are renowned for their high strength, superior corrosion resistance, excellent thermal stability, precise dimensional accuracy, and exceptional surface finish. Every product is manufactured to meet stringent quality standards, ensuring reliable performance across the most demanding industrial applications.
